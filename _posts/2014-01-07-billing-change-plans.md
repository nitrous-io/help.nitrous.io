### Upgrading Plans

When upgrading your subscription to a higher cost plan, it will be pro-rated based on the amount of unused time you have from the current billing month. You will be billed immediately and the billing date will adjust to the current day.

A simple example would be that if you upgraded from a $10/month plan to a $20/month plan in the middle of your billing cycle, you will be charged $15 for the first month of your new plan, and your next charge will be in a month (you receive an instant credit of $5 for unused time on the old plan).

**Pro-ration for upgrade: Price of new plan - Unused time from old plan**

### Downgrading Plans

If you downgrade plans then you will not receive pro-ration. On your next billing cycle you will be charged the full amount of the new plan you are using.

### What happens to my boxes if I cancel the subscription?

You will be able to retain a free box based on the remaining N2O balance after cancelation. Any additional boxes must be terminated, before cancelling the subscription.

However, any remaining balance will not refunded when you cancel a subscription.
