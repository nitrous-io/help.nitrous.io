The N2O you acquire through your subscription is yours to keep while you remain subscribed. You will only lose purchased N2O capacity if you cancel your subscription. 

You utilize N2O when you create boxes. When you terminate a box, the N2O is made available again for new boxes or upgrading existing boxes. You may also [reconfigure](/box-settings/) the memory and storage of each box in order to free up or utilize more N2O.
