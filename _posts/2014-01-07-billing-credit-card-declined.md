If your credit card has been declined, you may be able to fix this by re-entering your billing information or contacting your bank for assistance. [Sign in](https://www.nitrous.io/users/sign_in) to your Nitrous account and navigate to the [upgrade page](https://www.nitrous.io/app#/n2o/upgrade) to enter in your payment information.

### Avoidable Errors

If re-entering your credit card information doesn't solve the problem, there are a few avoidable errors to consider:

**AVS Errors:**
This can occur when the billing information you entered does not match what is on record with your bank. Check to ensure you are entering in the correct address which the bank has on file.

**Suspicious charges:**
Your bank may have acted proactively if an unauthorized charge was made. If this happens, you can lift the block by calling your bank and letting them know you are attempting to make a legitimate charge.

**Use a different card:**
If you are still unable to make a payment, consider using another card.

### Still not working?

Feel free to [contact us](mailto:support@nitrous.io) if you are still having issues making a payment.
